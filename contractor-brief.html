What we think should happen

Our working scope, in the order we'd build it. Tell us where this is wrong, what's missing, and what you'd sequence differently. The finish reference is 2435 Valley St, a 1908 cottage renovated with refinished oak, coved ceilings, an eat-in kitchen, and added skylights. That's the taste, not the budget.

1first

Stop the water

  • Tear off and replace the roof. New gutters and downspouts, attic vents, rotted sheathing replaced as found. Planchon bid $14.8kest. $18–20k
  • If we ever want skylights or a raised ceiling in the kitchen or living room, this is when. Worth pricing as an option.
2structure

Structure, while the crawlspace is open

  • Termite and dry rot repairs per the Section I findings: mudsill, cripple walls, subfloor, joists, bath floor, plus whatever the report undercounts. bid $29.9kest. $30–40k
  • Seismic: anchor bolts and plywood cripple-wall bracing. Berkeley refunds part of the transfer tax for this if done within a year of close. est. $12–17k net
  • Sewer: replace the upper lateral, add a cleanout, repair the three root-broken joints on the left side. lateral bid $5.5kest. $7–10k total
3systems

Systems, all at once

  • Full rewire. All knob-and-tube out, new 200 A panel, GFCI and AFCI where required, load sized for induction, heat pumps, and a future EV charger. est. $30–40k
  • Replumb supply and drains. Galvanized and the lead toilet drain both out. Rough-in for the kitchen in its new location and laundry in the rear porch. est. $20–28k
  • Heat: floor furnace removed and floor patched, mini-splits in. Water heater leaves the kitchen; a tankless unit goes in the laundry shed. est. $17–24k after rebates
  • Insulation and air sealing, attic and floor. Walls stay closed for now. est. $8–12k
4layout

Layout changes

  • Kitchen stays where it is (front-right, 8′-2″ × ≈12′-5″) and gets redone in place: the water-heater corner beside the range becomes counter. est. $35–55k with semi-custom or IKEA boxes, quartz, good appliances
  • Rear porch stays the laundry and becomes a proper shed: washer, dryer, and the tankless water heater in it; its only door stays on the east side. est. $8–15k
  • Living room, bedroom 2 (the rear-right room, which keeps its hall door and closet), bathroom, hall, and front porch keep their footprints.

Why the swap: we don't want to walk through a bedroom to get to the yard. It should cost little extra because every plumbing line is being replaced anyway and the sewer exits on the left side of the house. Tell us if that's wrong.

5finishes

Finishes

  • Bathroom full gut, same footprint unless the replumb makes a shift cheap. est. $25–35k
  • Plaster repair, interior doors, trim, paint throughout. est. $20–35k
  • Refinish the existing floors, patch where the furnace and walls come out, new floor in the kitchen and mudroom. est. $8–12k
  • Windows: repair, reglaze, and unstick only. No replacement in this pass. est. $6–12k
  • Deck: replace rotted members and stairs only. est. $5–8k
  • Drawings by a draftsperson plus a structural engineer, not a full architect. Berkeley permits and plan check. est. $20–35k combined
6later

Deferred to later years

  • Window replacement, exterior siding repair and paint, deck rebuild, yard, fence and driveway, fireplace and chimney work, wall insulation. If any of these can't wait, say so.
